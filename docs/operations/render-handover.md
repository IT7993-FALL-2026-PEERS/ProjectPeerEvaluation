### Render settings to know

- **Auto-deploy is off** on both services (`autoDeployTrigger: "off"` in `render.yaml`). Only CD deploys, through
  the deploy hooks.
- **Environment variables** on the backend: `NODE_VERSION`, `NODE_ENV=production`, `DEPLOY_ENV=staging`,
  `JWT_SECRET` (generated by Render), `MONGODB_URI`, `FRONTEND_URL`, `SMTP_HOST` (`sandbox.smtp.mailtrap.io`),
  `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS`, `SMTP_FROM`, `EMAIL_SEND_INTERVAL_MS`. The ones marked `sync: false` in
  `render.yaml` hold real secrets and live only in the Render dashboard. The frontend has `NODE_VERSION` and
  `REACT_APP_API_URL`.
- **Blueprint.** Changes to `render.yaml` take effect when the Blueprint syncs in Render.
- **Deploy hooks.** Each service has one (Settings > Deploy Hook). They're stored only as the
  `staging` environment secrets `RENDER_BACKEND_DEPLOY_HOOK_URL` and `RENDER_FRONTEND_DEPLOY_HOOK_URL` in GitHub.
  Anyone with a hook URL can deploy, so regenerate a hook if it leaks
  ([R7](rollback-and-recovery.md#r7-a-secret-leaked)).
- **Suspend and resume.** Staging stays running until final delivery (6 Dec 2026), because CD deploys to it after
  every merge that passes CI. Suspending both services is for the shut-down date (Option 2 below) or a team decision to change
  this. CD fails while they're suspended ([R3](rollback-and-recovery.md#r3-staging-doesnt-answer)).

## The decision: hand over or shut down

Decide at the Milestone 3 review (30 Nov 2026) at the latest, and finish before the final review (6 Dec 2026).
Ask the sponsor (Dr. Vyas) whether the department wants to keep a running staging environment.

### Option 1: hand over

The new owner (the sponsor, the department, or next year's team) takes over the accounts.

1. **Render.** A Hobby workspace cannot have other members, and Render's documentation describes no way to
   transfer a workspace to someone else ([Workspaces, Members, and Roles](https://render.com/docs/team-members)).
   So the usual route is for the new owner to create their own workspace, put the paid plan on their billing
   details, and deploy `render.yaml` as a new Blueprint there (step 2). Khoa then deletes the old workspace and
   removes his card. The other route is to upgrade this workspace to a plan that allows members, invite the new
   owner as Admin, and ask Render support whether the creator role can be handed over. Check which plan the
   workspace is on (Billing) before choosing.
2. **New workspace** (the usual route): copy the `sync: false` environment variables across (MongoDB, SMTP), generate new
   deploy hooks, and save them as the `staging` environment secrets in GitHub. If the service URLs change,
   update `FRONTEND_URL` and `REACT_APP_API_URL` in `render.yaml` and the URLs in `cd.yml`,
   `scripts/render-deploy.js` and `promote.yml`.
3. **Atlas and Mailtrap.** Add the new owner as a project owner, or move the data and credentials to their
   accounts and update `MONGODB_URI` and `SMTP_*` in Render.
4. **GitHub.** Make the new owner an organisation owner. Update the `production` environment's reviewers, and
   `CODEOWNERS`, if the people change.
5. **Prove it.** The new owner runs CD by hand with force and sees both services report the commit
   ([release procedure](release-procedure.md)).

### Option 2: shut down

1. Note the final live commits (`/api/health`, `/version.txt`) in the final delivery record below.
2. Export anything worth keeping from Atlas (Atlas > the cluster > Collections, or `mongodump`). Staging holds
   test data only.
3. **Render:** delete both services, delete the workspace, and remove the card.
4. **Atlas:** terminate the cluster. **Mailtrap:** delete the sandbox, or the account.
5. **GitHub:** delete the `staging` environment secrets. CD then fails, so disable the CD workflow
   (Actions > CD > ⋯ > Disable workflow) and say in the README that staging no longer runs. CI keeps working.
   Keep the `rc-*` and `release-*` releases: they're the delivery record.
